<Term id="chromatographie">Chromatography</Term> is a family of techniques that **separate the
components of a mixture** by their affinity for two phases: a moving *mobile* phase and a fixed
*stationary* phase.

<Callout type="definition">
  Each component travels at its own speed: those that "stick" most to the stationary phase move
  the slowest.
</Callout>

## Main families

- **Thin-layer / paper**: simple and cheap, ideal for teaching.
- **Gas chromatography**: for volatile compounds (drugs, fire accelerants).
- **Liquid chromatography (HPLC)**: for fragile or low-volatility molecules.

<Callout type="example">
  In forensic science, a sample's chromatogram is compared with those of reference substances to
  identify a product.
</Callout>
