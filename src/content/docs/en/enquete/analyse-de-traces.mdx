According to <Term id="principe-de-locard">Locard's exchange principle</Term>, every contact leaves
a trace. An investigation's job is to **find, collect and interpret** those traces.

<Callout type="warning">
  A badly collected or contaminated trace can lose all value as evidence.
</Callout>

## From collection to analysis

1. Locate and photograph the trace.
2. Collect it without contaminating it.
3. Analyse it in the lab (for example by chromatography).
4. Interpret the result with caution.
